## ✨ What Is qiaomu-clipper?

qiaomu-clipper is a "web clipper" — a small helper that sits in your browser and grabs the content of the page you are looking at. Instead of dumping raw HTML into a file, it tidies everything up into Markdown, the simple text format that Obsidian uses for all of its notes.

That means the article you clipped arrives as a normal note: headings, lists, quotes, links, and images all in place. You can highlight it, rewrite it, tag it, and link it to other notes exactly like anything else in your notes library.

The "AI chat" part is what makes it different from a plain clipper. Once a page is saved as a note, you can ask an AI assistant questions about it — summarise this, pull out the key points, explain this paragraph, turn this into a checklist. You read and think with the text instead of just storing it.

It also handles RSS feeds and "read later" style collecting, so your reading list and your notes live in the same place.

## 🛠️ Tips for a Smooth Experience

- **Clip the article, not the front page.** A clipper works best when the page actually contains the content — open the full article first.
- **Name your notes clearly.** A short, descriptive title makes search far more effective months later.
- **Add one line of context.** "Why I saved this" is the most valuable sentence in any note.
- **Clip into a dedicated folder** inside your notes library, at least at the start. It keeps things tidy while you build the habit.
- **Ask the AI specific questions.** "What are the three main arguments here?" gets a much better answer than "tell me about this."
- **Keep your notes library backed up.** Any folder you care about deserves a backup — synced folders and external drives both work.
